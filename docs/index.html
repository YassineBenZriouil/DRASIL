DRASIL

A living world for your files.

Drasil shows your folders and files as an interactive tree: folders are branches, files are leaves. See the shape of your whole drive, then zoom in and manage your files right there.

Windows Windows 10 and 11 · .exe installer macOS macOS 12 or later · Apple silicon or Intel · .dmg Linux .AppImage, .deb or .rpm

Why Drasil

  • See the big picture. Large and deeply nested folders stand out at a glance; folder sizes are worked out in the background.
  • A real file manager. Move, copy, rename and delete in the tree. Nothing is ever overwritten without asking, and deleting goes to the Trash.
  • Find anything. Search by name, filter by type, size or date, and highlight the largest or oldest files.
  • For everyone. Every action works from the keyboard, and a list view works with screen readers. Light and dark themes, and reduced motion.
  • Private. No account, no telemetry, no internet needed. Your files and their names never leave your computer. Privacy details.
